=== micro-cell

explain: He named them after the small rooms because that's what they reminded him of.

--- 💧

- Tiny flowers floating
+ Tiny things swimming
- Pieces of cork
hint: He saw living things moving around.
explain: He found tiny things swimming, which he called animalcules.

explain: The number comes from weighing organs and figuring out the typical size of cells, not by counting them all.

--- 🧵

- About 1 foot
- About 12 feet
hint: The fact box tells you the exact length when stretched out.
explain: The DNA is about 6 feet long when stretched out, but it's packed tightly inside the cell.

--- 🔋

term: mitochondria = The bean-shaped parts inside your cells that release energy from food.
fact: Muscle cells that work hard are packed with them; a heart muscle cell can be a third mitochondria by volume.
check: What do mitochondria do?
- Make the cell grow bigger
+ Release energy from food
- Carry instructions for the cell
hint: The first paragraph explains what they do.
explain: Mitochondria release energy from food, helping cells function.

--- 🧬
p: From a slice of cork in 1665 to the thirty trillion cells in you, each one is wrapped in a skin of fat, packed with DNA and tiny energy makers.
p: Not bad for something nobody could see until someone ground a good lens.

+ More than two hundred different viruses cause it

hint: Each virus causes similar symptoms, but there are many of them.
explain: There are more than two hundred different viruses that can cause a cold.

--- 🦠
p: Smaller than any bacterium, borrowing everything from the cell it gets into, and nobody agrees on whether it counts as alive.
